Processed here with ffmpeg: converted to mono 44.1 kHz WAV (Safari does not reliably
decode Ogg Vorbis), head silence trimmed, peak-normalised to -1.5 dBFS. Tick candidates
are additionally hard-trimmed to 140 ms with an 18 ms fade so they don't turn to mush
when the wheel is spinning fast.

snd/tick1-6.wav  <- Casino Audio: chips-collide-1..4, chip-lay-1..2
snd/thunk.wav    <- Impact Sounds: impactWood_heavy_000
snd/unlock.wav   <- Interface Sounds: confirmation_001

snd/lib/ holds every alternate take. Open sounds.html to audition them and pick
different ones — your choice saves to the browser and the app picks it up.
