Kenney's Interface Sounds pack was tried and then removed on purpose: those are
synthesized UI blips, not recordings, and the melodic ones stuck out badly. Everything
here now is a real recorded object — plastic chips, cards, wood and metal impacts.

Processed with ffmpeg: mono 44.1 kHz WAV (Safari does not reliably decode Ogg Vorbis),
head silence trimmed, peak-normalised to -1.5 dBFS. Tick candidates are additionally
hard-trimmed to 140 ms with an 18 ms fade so they don't turn to mush at wheel speed.

snd/tick1-6.wav  <- Casino Audio: chips-collide-1..4, chip-lay-1..2
snd/thunk.wav    <- Impact Sounds: impactWood_heavy_000
snd/unlock.wav   <- Casino Audio: chips-stack-1

snd/lib/ holds every alternate take. Open sounds.html to audition them and pick
different ones — your choice saves to the browser and the app picks it up.
