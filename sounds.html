SOUND PICKER

Tap any name to hear it. Tick choices toggle — pick up to 6 and the wheel round-robins them. Then hit SAVE and reopen the app.

TICK

Fires once per slice. Use SPIN TEST — a tick that's fine on its own can turn to mush at speed.

STOP

One hit when the wheel locks.

GOT IT

Plays when you tap GOT IT at the end of a hunt.

FAILED

And when you tap FAILED.

UNLOCK

Plays when you've walked far enough and the first wheel frees up.
